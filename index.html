A limestone face reconstructed in 3D from 103 drone photos.

Textured mesh

Light9.8 MB 366k triangles. Loads in a second, fine on a phone. Balanced47.6 MB 1.7M triangles. The one to look at first. Full detail95.4 MB 3.4M triangles and an 8192×3932 texture.

Gaussian splat

Original splat73 MB The raw radiance field — softer and hazier off-axis, but it keeps the fine detail and the view-dependent light that the mesh flattens away.

Built from a Kaggle dataset of 103 × 8000×4500 drone photos: COLMAP for camera poses, then 3D Gaussian Splatting trained on a Tesla P100, then Poisson surface reconstruction into the meshes above. Both notebooks live in kaggle/ in the repo.
